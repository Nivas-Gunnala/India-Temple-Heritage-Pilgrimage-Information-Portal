BEFORE YOU SET OUT

A few thoughtful
travelling habits.

01

Check current guidance

Look up official temple notices for access, darshan, photography, and opening schedules.

02

Dress and act with respect

Follow local dress guidance, ask before photographing people, and observe signs around worship spaces.

03

Leave space in the plan

Allow for queues, weather, local events, and rest. A slower visit can be a more meaningful one.
